GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Answered.

Here are a few things clients often ask before getting started.

Ask me something else ↗
What do I get when I buy a website? ＋

The exact deliverables depend on the package we agree on. We will confirm pages, features, editable files and any setup support in writing before starting.

Can you customize a design for my brand? ＋

Yes. Colors, text, images, branding and other changes can be discussed. Larger feature requests may change the price and timeline.

Are domain and hosting included? ＋

Unless specifically included in your written quote, domain registration, hosting, paid tools and third-party services are separate costs.

Can you help me put the website online? ＋

Yes, launch guidance or deployment support can be discussed when we confirm your project scope.

How do I place an order? ＋

Add a design to your cart and send the enquiry through WhatsApp, or contact me directly by email. Your order is confirmed only after we agree on scope, price and payment terms.
